# Ganpan: signage for AI agents

This is the entrance of the sign for Ganpan, a convention for pages that an owner puts up for a guest's AI to read. Owner: Seunghun Lee. Draft v0.1, not validated in the field.

## Open the whole sign

AI apps open an address the guest sends as text. Some open the addresses below by themselves, and some open them only after the guest sends them. Sending this block once opens every piece.

```
{{sign}}/what
{{sign}}/rules
{{sign}}/make
{{sign}}/status
{{sign}}/faq
```

People can copy a ready-made first message for their AI at <{{sign}}/start>.

## Pieces

- What it is: <{{sign}}/what>. The concept, three scenes of use, why the owner builds no AI.
- Principles: <{{sign}}/rules>. The seven principles and the reason for each.
- How to put up a sign: <{{sign}}/make>. The authoring guide: addresses, what goes where, how to write, the placard.
- Where it stands: <{{sign}}/status>. What is confirmed, what is not, where the idea came from.
- Frequently asked questions: <{{sign}}/faq>. llms.txt and MCP, cost, QR codes, the name, contact.

The full convention, for people: <{{base}}/spec> (Korean: <{{base}}/ko/spec>). Contact: <https://github.com/engmung/ganpan/issues>

Owner's pick: on a first visit, start with "What it is".
